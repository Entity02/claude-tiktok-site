Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Claude TikTok MCP is a personal service that connects the account owner's TikTok account with Claude through authorized TikTok APIs.

Information accessed

When authorized by the account owner, the service may access basic account information and information about the owner's TikTok videos, including video metadata and available video statistics.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the requested functionality, such as checking published videos and analyzing their performance.

Data sharing

Information is not sold or shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the requested service, including TikTok APIs and Claude.

Data retention

The service does not intentionally retain TikTok information longer than necessary to provide its functionality.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the service owner.
